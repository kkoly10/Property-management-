# Crecy Marketing Image Direction

Status: approved working direction for the lean marketing redesign.

## Purpose

The marketing experience should show the relationship between a real place, the people who care for it, and the Crecy product that coordinates the work. Photography is evidence and atmosphere, not decoration. It should sit beside real Crecy interface proof rather than replace it.

Maple Court is a fictional Crecy Living demonstration community. It must never be described as a real operator property, customer, or case study.

## Visual canon

- Contemporary North American mid-rise rental community.
- Warm red-brown brick, light limestone, matte charcoal metal, large practical windows, and mature green landscaping.
- Natural oak, warm off-white stone, charcoal details, restrained greenery, and durable interior finishes.
- Natural editorial realism with soft daylight, visible material texture, and slight lived-in imperfection.
- Premium through care and specificity, not exaggerated luxury.
- Wide landscape compositions with safe responsive crops and room for interface overlays where useful.
- No visible logos, signage, fabricated labels, identifiable residents, or staged lifestyle models.

## Composition rules

1. Pair photography with an actual Crecy workflow, record, or interface state. Do not place floating generic UI cards over every image.
2. Use one dominant image per narrative chapter. Avoid image grids that turn the community into stock-photo decoration.
3. Keep Crecy OS primarily operational and data-led. The maintenance image may establish context, but the product proof must remain the operating workspace.
4. Security remains architecture- and controls-led; it does not need lifestyle photography.
5. On mobile, retain place identity and the human action. Do not default to a centered subject crop that removes the property context.

## Accessibility copy

- Exterior: `Maple Court's brick and limestone residential entrance in late-afternoon light.`
- Lobby: `The Maple Court lobby with a reception desk, resident seating, and garden-facing entry.`
- Model home: `A Maple Court apartment with an open kitchen, living room, and balcony.`
- Maintenance: `A maintenance technician inspecting the plumbing beneath a Maple Court kitchen sink.`

## Adversarial acceptance

The set is acceptable only while it continues to read as one believable fictional community, avoids impossible architectural or human details, supports useful mobile crops, and makes the adjacent Crecy workflow more legible. If the photography could be swapped for arbitrary luxury real-estate imagery without changing the story, the composition has failed.
