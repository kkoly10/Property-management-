# Counsel and Compliance Launch Checklist

A checked item requires a named owner and evidence link. Unchecked items do not block coding/staging; they block affected production behavior.

## Global corporate/product

- [ ] Contracting entity confirmed
- [ ] Operator and portal Terms approved
- [ ] Global Privacy Notice and DPA approved
- [ ] Subprocessor/cookie/AUP documents published
- [ ] E-sign, payment, subscription/cancellation disclosures approved
- [ ] Accessibility, AI data-use, and document-responsibility notices approved

## Privacy operations

- [ ] Privacy owner, data inventory/classification, and controller/processor map complete
- [ ] Rights, export, deletion, retention, and legal-hold workflows tested
- [ ] Consent ledger and marketing suppression tested
- [ ] Subprocessor-change procedure approved

## Security

- [ ] Written program, MFA, and step-up authentication approved/tested
- [ ] Cross-tenant RLS and platform-admin audit tests pass
- [ ] File scanning, secret rotation, backup restoration, incident tabletop complete
- [ ] Independent penetration test complete
- [ ] Vulnerability disclosure channel and cyber insurance active

## Payments

- [ ] Connected operator confirmed as merchant of record
- [ ] Direct charges and hosted onboarding implemented
- [ ] Webhook signature, idempotency, refund, return, and dispute behavior tested
- [ ] Responsibilities disclosed; deposits excluded
- [ ] PCI path confirmed and raw card data absent

## United States

- [ ] State privacy/breach applicability reviewed
- [ ] California flow where applicable
- [ ] CAN-SPAM, SMS/telephone, E-SIGN, renewal/cancellation reviewed
- [ ] Screening disabled pending FCRA review
- [ ] Tax/nexus assessment complete

## Canada

- [ ] PIPEDA/provincial routing, meaningful consent, breach, and CASL reviewed
- [ ] English/French parity and Quebec commercial/privacy review complete
- [ ] Cross-border disclosure and GST/HST assessment complete

## Mexico

- [ ] 2025 privacy memo and Spanish notices approved
- [ ] ARCO, PROFECO, cancellation, and complaint routes tested
- [ ] SAT/VAT/CFDI strategy approved
- [ ] Stripe Mexico capability verified

## Accessibility/localization

- [ ] WCAG 2.2 AA audit, keyboard, and screen-reader tests complete
- [ ] Accessible-document strategy approved
- [ ] Required English/Spanish/French QA and legal translation review complete

## Production approval

- [ ] Product, engineering, security, privacy, payment, counsel, tax, and support/incident sign-offs complete
